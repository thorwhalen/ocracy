ocracy.services

Service layer: three tiers of access to backends.

  • ServiceCollection — a lazy mapping backend_id -> ServiceHandle, also reachable by attribute (services.tesseract).

  • ServiceHandle — a per-backend namespace exposing the normalized read method, the backend’s config/info, and the raw adapter.

This gives three escalating levels of control:

ocracy.ocr(img)                          # 1. simple facade (default backend)
ocracy.services.tesseract.read(img, lang="fra")   # 2. pick a backend explicitly
ocracy.services.tesseract.adapter        # 3. raw engine adapter, full control

Mirrors the sibling denote facade’s service layer.
